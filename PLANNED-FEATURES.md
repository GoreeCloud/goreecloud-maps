## Additional incomplete capability areas

- rich POI/place data, nearby/category discovery, and place-detail experiences;
- route alternatives, advanced route constraints, traffic-aware estimates, turn-by-turn navigation, and rerouting;
- traffic, closures, incidents, transit, terrain, 3D/globe, licensed imagery, indoor mapping, and EV routing;
- offline search/routing packages, storage quota, package freshness, integrity, updates, and safe expiry;
- complete Saved Places and collection synchronization;
- governed invitation lookup/delivery, full membership UI, revocation, share links, ownership transfer, annotations, and shared route plans;
- GoreeCloud Search interoperability where an explicit geographic discovery contract is accepted;
- production Identity client/session acceptance and production PostGIS/RLS acceptance;
- Everkeep export/backup/restore/portability and clean-environment recovery;
- Privacy Shield/Wardveil/Policy/Observability runtime acceptance;
- supported native/form-factor clients where justified.
